Accessibility tools ask the student to keep reading the same shape, harder.
One shape fits nobody
Larger type and text-to-speech help, and every student gets the same one. Devon and Mara need opposite things from the same paragraph — and neither of them needs a bigger font.
The student is the signal
Onboarding asks what trips you up, never what you have been diagnosed with. After that, tapping This isn’t working is the whole interface — and the planner has to do something specific with it.
Traceable enough to assign
An instructor will not hand a class an AI rewrite of the textbook they are examined on. Claim-level tracing is what makes this adoptable instead of a novelty.
The text stays exactly as authoritative as it was. Only the way in changes.
