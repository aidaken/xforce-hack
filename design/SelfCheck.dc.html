Nothing lost, nothing invented.
A remake is only worth anything if the student can still be examined on it. Here is what enforces that today — and the part we have not earned yet.
Shipped · in the prompt
The model is held to the passage
The remake prompt forbids inventing facts and requires scientific terms survive verbatim — a checklist may not turn oxidative phosphorylation into “the last bit”. Every remake opens by naming the format and the reason.
Shipped · on the screen
A human reads them side by side
The original never leaves the Reading view. A teammate reads source against remake and signs off before we call a reading done. That is the check the brief asked for, and it is the one that actually ran.
Not shipped — and we will say so
The counters are seeded, not computed
The Reading screen renders a supported / missing / invented panel, but those flags are local seed data. There is no server claim map behind them yet. We are not going to show a judge a number our backend did not produce.
The two failures the human is reading for
Lost — the scaffold goes
Summarisers drop the sentence that frames the rest. Cut “the pathway has three tightly linked stages” and a student can finish every step without the structure the exam is actually asking for.
Invented — the plausible number
The passage says a small net gain and most of the ATP is made here. It never counts. A model that helpfully supplies “38 ATP per glucose” has taught something the source did not say.
