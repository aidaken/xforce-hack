Source

Cellular respiration releases energy from glucose in three linked stages: glycolysis, the citric acid cycle, and oxidative phosphorylation. Most ATP is made in the electron transport chain, with carbon dioxide and water as waste.

ADHD remake

  1. Break glucose into pyruvate.
  2. Run the citric acid cycle.
  3. Use the electron transport chain.
  4. Collect ATP. Waste: CO2 + water.

Same facts. Two working memories.

ADHD — one step at a time

  1. Do this next: glycolysis.
  2. Then: citric acid cycle.
  3. Then: electron transport chain.
  4. Check: ATP is the point, not extra story.

Dyslexia — short labels, big type

Glucose → Pyruvate → Krebs → ETC → ATP

Keep the real terms. Don’t rename pyruvate.

The agent has to choose, check, and adapt.

  1. Plan. Is this a process, a rule system, or a pile of definitions? The rationale stays on screen.
  2. Remake. Checklist, flowchart, or a short quest — not a smaller wall of text.
  3. Check. Every node maps to a source span. Invented or missing facts light up.
  4. Adapt. “This isn’t working” switches format instead of repeating the same prose.
