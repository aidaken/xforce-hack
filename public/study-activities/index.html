BIOLOGY / CELLULAR ENERGY

How cells release energy

01 Glycolysis takes place in the cytoplasm. One glucose molecule is split into two pyruvate molecules, producing a net gain of two ATP and two NADH.

02 In eukaryotic cells, pyruvate oxidation and the citric acid cycle occur in the mitochondrial matrix. Carbon dioxide is released, and electron carriers NADH and FADH₂ are produced.

03 The electron transport chain is located in the inner mitochondrial membrane. Electrons from NADH and FADH₂ help establish a proton gradient that drives ATP synthase. Oxygen is the final electron acceptor and water is formed.
