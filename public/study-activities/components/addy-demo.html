Cellular respiration: checklist

  1. Glucose enters the cell and is split in the cytoplasm (glycolysis).
  2. Pyruvate moves into the mitochondria.
  3. The citric acid cycle strips off electrons and releases CO₂.
  4. The electron transport chain pumps protons across the membrane.
  5. ATP synthase uses that gradient to make ATP.

Why it matters

Every cell in your body runs this loop constantly. When you sprint, your muscle cells burn through glucose faster than oxygen can arrive, so they fall back on fermentation and you feel the burn.

Think of the mitochondrion as a tiny hydroelectric dam: the electron transport chain pumps water (protons) uphill, and ATP synthase is the turbine that spins as it flows back down.
